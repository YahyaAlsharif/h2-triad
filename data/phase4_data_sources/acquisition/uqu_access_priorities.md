# UQU manual-access priorities

Prepared 2026-09-13 from the [acquisition log](acquisition_log.csv), [active missing-paper queue](papers_no_pdf.txt), [original acquisition guide](../../../info/phase4_dataset_acquisition_guide.md), and [acquisition report](acquisition_report.md). No new literature research, authentication, downloads, scientific extraction, or access-status changes were performed.

This is an editorial ranking of the existing candidates, not a second active queue. Titles, DOIs and journals come from the log; design descriptions come from the guide. FREE-17, originally ranked 9, was subsequently acquired and verified and is therefore removed from the active recommendations without renumbering the historical ranks. All remaining ranked parent articles are missing locally and not full-text verified. Treat the described controls, variation and measurement coverage as acquisition leads requiring confirmation against the eventual full text; comparability across separate papers is not established.

The first five prioritize Fe/Ni/carbon comparisons, a broad sulfide comparison, direct halide evidence and Ni particle-size variation. The next active recommendations add encapsulated FeNi, oxyfluorides, supported Fe, and an N-rich Ni support. Current coverage has 7 of 16 core papers filed under oxides; 15 are from Journal of Magnesium and Alloys and FREE-17 is from Transactions of Nonferrous Metals Society of China. Folder counts are organizational, not an exhaustive chemical classification. Non-JMA primary papers can broaden source coverage but do not by themselves remove selection bias.

## Deferred and already available

FREE-1, FREE-2, FREE-3, FREE-14, FREE-16, FREE-17 and EXISTING-A are already available and should not consume missing-paper acquisition effort. Available extended EXISTING-C does not establish results for direct MgH2 + CaF2. None of these sources establishes measurements for the project's proposed combined Fe/Ni + N-C + CaF2 formulation.

After obtaining any parent article, verify identity, completeness and experimental scope before changing its acquisition status. Keep any associated supplement linked to its parent; a supplement alone does not complete the parent record.

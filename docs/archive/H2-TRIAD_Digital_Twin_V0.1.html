Testable proposition

Does adjacency improve kinetics at equal loading?

Onset temperatureActivation energy · EₐKinetics · t₉₀Reversible capacityCycling stability

Support the hypothesis if…

Replicated 4S preparations consistently improve independent kinetic metrics versus matched 3S, with distinct adjacency confirmed by microscopy and confounders controlled.

Reject or revise it if…

There is no reproducible advantage within measurement uncertainty, or apparent gains are explained by particle size, surface area, milling, or phase differences. Do not force a positive conclusion.

Characterization & measurement plan

Use TEM / STEM-EDS to check Fe–Ni and Ca/F adjacency; XRD for phases, XPS for chemical states, and BET for surface-area differences. Repeat characterization after cycling because the interface may reconstruct.

Match onset heating rate and instrument; use multiple heating rates and a declared method for Eₐ. Measure t₉₀ at fixed T/P, report the capacity denominator, and use independent repeat preparations. PCI/PCT and van’t Hoff analysis are needed to investigate thermodynamics.

Control ladder & screening targets

Include processing-matched MgH₂, MgH₂ + N-C, Fe–Ni/N-C, and MgH₂ + CaF₂ controls alongside 3S and 4S. The report suggests paired 5, 7.5, and 10 wt% loadings where resources permit.

Targets, not results Onset ≤250 °C; ≥5.5 wt% capacity at ≤10 wt% additive; ≥90% retention after 50 cycles. Predefine repeat count, endpoints, and decision criteria with the lab.

Equations, units & excluded effects

Q̇ = ṅH₂ × ΔH. Convert H₂ mass flow with 2.016 g/mol and 3,600 s/h: Q̇ (kW) = rate (kg/h) × 1,000 / 2.016 × ΔH (kJ/mol) / 3,600. At 75 kJ/mol this is ≈10.33 kWh/kg H₂, consistent with the report’s ≈10.3.

Inventory (kg H₂) = composite bed mass × C_upper / 100. Ideal duration (h) = inventory / requested rate. Charging assumes complete conversion of an initially depleted equivalent inventory; discharging assumes full initial inventory.

Excludes sensible heating, thermal losses, spatial gradients, incomplete conversion, shell/fins mass, and balance of plant. Future work must solve the report’s lumped balance with explicit charge/discharge sign convention, measured heat capacity, UA, boundary temperatures, losses, and kinetics. No claim is made that CaF₂ lowers ΔH.

The evidence gap that remains open

The report found insufficient convincing direct evidence for CaF₂ in the proposed catalytic MgH₂ role. YF₃ + Ni/C is supporting evidence for a different fluoride, not validation of CaF₂. It found no exact published Fe–Ni ∥ CaF₂ / N-C match and no direct 3S/4S result in its search. Those negative findings are scoped to that research pass, not proof of global absence or patent novelty.

\section*{Executive Summary}

H2-TRIAD combines a falsifiable catalyst-development concept with an implemented, evidence-conscious Digital Twin. The material hypothesis is a deliberately pre-assembled Fe-Ni/N-C/\CaFtwo{} interface: Fe-Ni nanoparticles and \CaFtwo{} nanodomains are spatially anchored on an N-doped porous-carbon scaffold before the catalyst is integrated with \MgHtwo{}. The novelty claim is therefore the engineered interface and its validation strategy, not the mere presence of familiar components.

The decisive laboratory comparison is S4, the pre-assembled architecture, versus S3, an equal-composition physical mixture prepared and tested under identical conditions. Fe-Ni has strong precedent as a kinetic promoter, N-C can support dispersion and transport, and fluoride-containing systems motivate interfacial study. However, \CaFtwo{} is treated as a testable modifier, not as a proven route to lower the thermodynamic enthalpy of \MgHtwo{}. Any thermodynamic claim requires pressure-composition-temperature data and van't Hoff analysis.

The software prototype supports this program by preserving literature provenance, returning exact measurements before model estimates, rejecting unsupported inputs, and exposing uncertainty. Its current evidence base contains 119 accessible literature observations; 109 observations from 16 papers and 36 samples enter a bounded CatBoost capacity model. Paper-held-out evaluation gives a mean absolute error of 1.1693 \wtpct{} \Htwo{} and $R^{2}=0.3314$. These are actual software-evaluation results, not measurements of S4. The proposed numerical catalyst thresholds are design targets to be accepted, revised, or rejected experimentally.

\evidencebox{Submission claim: H2-TRIAD is a working digital research aid and a proposed catalyst architecture. No laboratory performance is claimed for the Fe-Ni/N-C/\CaFtwo{} interface.}

\section{Challenge and Project Objective}

\MgHtwo{} is attractive because magnesium is abundant and the hydride offers a theoretical material-level hydrogen capacity of about 7.6 \wtpct{}. Its practical limitations include strong Mg-H thermodynamics, slow molecular-hydrogen activation at the surface, slow solid-state transport, and poor heat transfer. Pure or lightly processed \MgHtwo{} therefore commonly requires elevated temperatures and long reaction times \cite{webb2015,jain2010}.

The project objective is to develop and validate a noble-metal-free, interface-engineered Fe-Ni/N-C/\CaFtwo{} catalyst that accelerates reversible absorption and desorption, preserves useful composite capacity, and resists agglomeration over cycling. Kinetic improvement and any genuine thermodynamic change will be evaluated separately. Lower onset temperature by itself will not be interpreted as proof of a lower reaction enthalpy.

\section{Concept and Candidate Novelty}

The final candidate material is \MgHtwo{} combined with a \emph{pre-assembled Fe-Ni/N-C/\CaFtwo{} interface architecture}. The first comparison materials are controls, not competing project ideas. The central hypothesis is that co-locating the three functions creates persistent reaction boundaries and shorter transport paths that cannot be reproduced reliably by random contact after milling.

\section{Implemented Digital Twin}

The Digital Twin is a literature-grounded predictive workspace for condition-aware comparison of \MgHtwo{} experiments. Its scientific decision rule is:

\begin{center}
  \color{HydraTeal}\bfseries \resultflow
\end{center}

For a verified sample at matching recorded conditions, the system returns the exact literature measurement with its qualifier and provenance. If no exact measurement exists, the request may be evaluated by the capacity model only after numeric and chemistry support checks. A supported prediction includes an empirical interval, model identity, and warnings. Outside the documented domain, the response is \emph{unavailable} and contains no fabricated capacity. Missing conditions are not wildcards, distinct measurements are not averaged into synthetic observations, and unavailable does not mean zero.

\section{Scientific Data and Predictive Model}

The read-only scientific index joins canonical source, sample, and measurement records. Its 119 accessible ordinary absorption/desorption observations retain source identity, sample and preparation context, conditions, capacity basis, qualifiers, and source locators. Approximate and threshold values remain qualified, and reported room temperature is not converted into an invented number. Historical synthetic/demo records remain in a separate SQLite dataset and do not enter literature lookup or model training.

The capacity-training view contains 109 usable scalar observations from 16 independent papers and 36 samples. The compact CatBoost regressor uses measurement mode, temperature, duration, catalyst loading, catalyst presence and component count, and support context. Chemistry and pressure also participate in eligibility checks. Evaluation uses leave-one-paper-out grouping so correlated rows from one publication cannot enter both training and testing.

\section{Material Route and Controlled Validation}

Prepare an N-doped porous-carbon scaffold, anchor nanoscale Fe-Ni at its active sites, introduce spatially dispersed \CaFtwo{} domains, and integrate the pre-assembled catalyst with \MgHtwo{} by controlled milling under inert atmosphere. Fe:Ni ratio, total catalyst loading, \CaFtwo{} fraction, scaffold properties, and milling energy are optimization variables rather than fixed claims.

\subsection{Proposed go/no-go targets}

These thresholds guide experimental design; none is a reported or model-predicted S4 result.

\begin{itemize}
  \item S4 reduces time to 90\% conversion by at least 50\% versus both S2 and S3 at identical conditions.
  \item Desorption onset is at or below 250\,$^{\circ}$C, or at least 75\,$^{\circ}$C below S0 under the same heating protocol.
  \item Reversible composite capacity is at least 5.5 \wtpct{} \Htwo{} with total catalyst loading at or below 10 \wtpct{}.
  \item At least 90\% of initial reversible capacity remains after 50 cycles, with replicate testing and uncertainty reported.
  \item A statistically significant architecture or component-interaction effect is observed; otherwise, the synergy claim is rejected.
\end{itemize}

\section{Computational and AI Support}

Computational work is an experiment-guidance layer, not replacement evidence. DFT can compare hydrogen adsorption/dissociation, vacancy formation, charge transfer, and diffusion barriers in simplified \MgHtwo{}/Fe-Ni, \MgHtwo{}/N-C, \MgHtwo{}/\CaFtwo{}, and combined-interface models. Data-driven barrier models demonstrate how lower-cost descriptors can prioritize calculations, but their predictions still require experimental confrontation \cite{li2024}.

After measured S0--S4 data exist, a small-data Bayesian optimizer can recommend the next Fe:Ni ratio, catalyst loading, \CaFtwo{} fraction, or milling condition. Each plotted curve must be labeled as a measured result, literature benchmark, or model prediction. The current CatBoost model explores capacity trends in the curated literature; it does not predict that the proposed S4 architecture will meet the targets above.

For supported configurations, the deployed application can evaluate a temperature-by-loading grid while holding other inputs fixed. Every cell is screened before inference; unsupported cells remain null and are excluded from the surface.

\section{System Architecture and Current Validation}

H2-TRIAD uses a React/Vite frontend and FastAPI backend. The backend connects the interface to canonical literature tables and a cached CatBoost predictor for scientific results, while a separate SQLite store supports the explicitly labeled historical synthetic/demo explorer. A repository-root Dockerfile builds the frontend and serves the bundled application and \texttt{/api} routes through one Uvicorn process on Railway.

Release validation currently records 22 model tests, 72 backend tests, and 26 browser tests, including the integrated container workflow. Checks cover exact-measurement precedence, qualified values, unavailable responses without numeric fields, landscape masking, scientific dataset integrity, and stable production artifacts. This establishes a working software prototype, not a validated physical catalyst.

\section{Hackathon Demonstrator, Value, and Risk}

The demonstration links the implemented software to the next experimental decision. A judge can configure a condition-aware \MgHtwo{} comparison, inspect an exact literature result or bounded model estimate, review its provenance and uncertainty, and then examine the S0--S4 validation plan. A physical display can use a sealed cartridge cutaway and non-reactive material mock-up; no live hydrogen is required.

The likely first application context is stationary storage, renewable buffering, or a heat-integrated system. The concept avoids noble metals and uses broadly available Fe, Ni, carbon, and \CaFtwo{}, but nanostructure processing, inert handling, oxidation control, and heat management still require technical and economic assessment. High heat demand may limit mobile use, so no vehicle-readiness claim is made.

Real powder synthesis and pressure testing must occur only in a qualified laboratory with inert handling, approved pressure equipment, ventilation, hydrogen detection, and documented risk review.

\section{Limitations}

\begin{itemize}
  \item The exact pre-assembled Fe-Ni/N-C/\CaFtwo{} architecture has not been synthesized or tested by this project.
  \item Evidence for \CaFtwo{} is indirect and composition-dependent; inactivity, adverse phases, or capacity dilution remain plausible outcomes.
  \item The literature dataset is small at the independent-paper level and concentrated in particular catalyst families.
  \item Paper-held-out performance is moderate, and broad empirical intervals expose substantial uncertainty.
  \item Feature-wise support checks do not prove joint experimental coverage, causal optimization, or mechanistic validity.
  \item The exact combination's patentability is unconfirmed pending a formal prior-art review.
\end{itemize}

\section{Conclusion}

H2-TRIAD advances a coherent hackathon proposition: a deliberately pre-assembled Fe-Ni/N-C/\CaFtwo{} interface to be tested through a decisive S4-versus-S3 control, paired with a working Digital Twin that keeps literature observations, bounded predictions, uncertainty, and unsupported regions visibly distinct. The proposal is scientifically valuable precisely because it is falsifiable. If S4 does not outperform the equal-composition mixture, the interface-synergy claim is rejected; if equilibrium measurements do not change, the system remains correctly described as a kinetic catalyst. Computational and AI tools prioritize experiments, while laboratory measurements retain the final evidentiary authority.
